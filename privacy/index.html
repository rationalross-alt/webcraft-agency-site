Privacy notice

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

WebCraft Consulting is a trading name of Ross Davidson, who is responsible for your personal information (the "data controller"). You can contact us at Ross.Davidson@webcraftconsulting.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through our enquiry form or by email: your name, business name, email address, phone number and your message.
  • Business information that is already public, such as a company's registered name and address on Companies House, or its public business listing, which we may use to prepare a website preview for that business.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics that identify you.

How we use it and why

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to build and discuss your website preview. Our lawful basis is your request (taking steps before a contract) and our legitimate interest in responding.
  • To provide our services if you become a customer. Our lawful basis is the contract with you.
  • To contact businesses about our services using public business contact details. Our lawful basis is legitimate interest. Every such message identifies us clearly and lets you opt out, and we keep a do-not-contact list so we never contact you again once you ask us not to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of trusted providers to run our business, such as our email provider (Microsoft) and our website hosts (GitHub and Cloudflare). They only process information on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept for as long as we provide services to you and then for up to 6 years for legal and accounting reasons. Do-not-contact requests are kept for as long as needed to honour them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Just email us and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
