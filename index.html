Common questions

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We build a working preview of your new website at no cost and with no obligation. You only pay if you decide to go ahead.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you do, where you work and how customers reach you. Photos and reviews help, but we can start without them and add them later.

How long does it take to go live?

Once you approve your preview and send any final details, most sites go live within a few working days.

Do I need a web address already?

No. If you have one, we use it. If not, we will help you choose and set one up.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

The £99 a month plan has a 3 month minimum. After that it runs month to month and you can cancel with a month's notice.

What counts as a small change?

Things like new opening hours, prices, photos, a new service or a seasonal offer. Bigger additions, such as new pages, are quoted up front.
